# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 19 August 2026

Chess Brain Trainer does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not
collect personal data such as your name, e-mail address or contacts.

## What stays on your device

Everything the app records about your practice — puzzles played, results, XP,
ranks, achievements and settings — is stored only on your device. It never
leaves it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to report crashes and errors. A
report contains the device model, the Android and app version, and the technical
details of the crash. It does not identify you personally and is used only to
find and fix faults.

## Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. Google is the seller;
we never receive or store your payment details.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and contains no ads and no links to external
content.
